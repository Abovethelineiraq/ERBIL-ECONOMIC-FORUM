A platform for economic dialogue

Organised by Above The Line in collaboration with the Kurdistan Regional Government’s Ministry of Trade and Industry, the Erbil Economic Forum brings together government representatives, business leaders, investors and key stakeholders.

It exists to foster economic dialogue, promote investment and strengthen cooperation between the public and private sectors, so that decisions are made with both sides in the room.
